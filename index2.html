Project 1: NYPD Arrests 2021 ⤏ Learn More

My team and I analyzed 155,000+ NYPD arrest records (2021) using Python (pandas), Excel, and Tableau to clean, transform, and visualize key crime trends. The data revealed borough concentrations, seasonal spikes, demographic disparities, gender gaps in arrests and insights that informed actionable recommendations for public safety.

Project 2: Spotify ⤏ Learn More

Worked with my peers on a group assignment on Spotify. Our objective was to see how much users are on Spotify by sending out surveys and interviewing some of the people on their thoughts on Spotify premium if they will purchase it or just continue with the free plan. The respondents gave suggestions on how Spotify can improve and what features it excels at.

Project 3: AT&T ⤏ Learn More

For this project I was working with AT&T (Paragon One) and the purpose of this assignment was to see how AT&T can target Gen Z consumers. This project shows the suggestions I implemented with KPI metrics. I conducted case studies with other stadiums and see what they did to match AT&T stadium.

Project 4: AXE ⤏ Learn More

For our last assignment, I worked to rebrand AXE and make it more appealing to a broader audience. AXE is more appealing to teenage boys, and my goal was to broaden that demographic to include college students and young professionals as well. AXE is notorious for its terrible odor. I proposed concepts and a rebranding strategy to make AXE's reputation disappear.
